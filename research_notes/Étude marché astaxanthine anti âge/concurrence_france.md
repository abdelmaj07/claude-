## 1. Qui vend de l'astaxanthine aux consommateurs français ? (tableau concurrents, dosage 12 mg vs 4/6/8 mg)

### Takeaway
No French DTC or pharmacy brand we found makes astaxanthin its hero product. It is one SKU among dozens at generalist labs: Nutrimuscle, Nutrixeal, Nutrimea, Cuure, Supersmart, Anastore, Vit'all+, Solgar, Prozis and Nutriforce. All of them dose at 4-8 mg, usually with lab, sport or antioxidant branding. The 12 mg segment is served almost only by foreign or unbranded Amazon.fr sellers (Nu U Nutrition, VivoNu, "Depot"-type German listings, DW) and by Sunday Natural (DE). That matches the EU novel-food cap of 8 mg/day for adults: the market's "serious" brands stop at 8 mg, and Nutrixeal even sells 8 mg as "the maximum authorised". This is a key strategic and regulatory fact for Nohancy's 12 mg.

**E. Regulatory frame on dose (explains the 4/6/8 mg clustering)**
- EU novel food "astaxanthin-rich oleoresin from Haematococcus pluvialis": the maximum authorised intake in food supplements is **8 mg astaxanthin/day for people over 14**, 5.7 mg for ages 10 to under 14, and 2.3 mg for ages 3 to under 10. Governing texts: Implementing Regulations (EU) 2017/2470 (Union list), **2021/1377** (change of conditions of use) and **2023/1581** — [agrinfo.eu](https://agrinfo.eu/book-of-reports/novel-food-astaxanthin-rich-oleoresin-from-algae/pdf/), [EUR-Lex 2023/1581](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2023/1581/oj/eng), [EUR-Lex 2021/1377](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2021/1377/oj/eng), [EFSA 2020 opinion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7448075/)
- EFSA published a further 2025 opinion on extending the uses of this oleoresin. Its content was not captured — [EFSA 2025](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.2903/j.efsa.2025.9737)

## 5. White space (positionnements non revendiqués ou mal exécutés)

### Takeaway
No one owns "astaxanthin as a premium, beauty-first, single-hero brand" in France. The ingredient is scattered across generalist labs (lab/sport/antioxidant codes), pharmacy brands ("Solaire - Beauté") and anonymous Amazon 12 mg listings. The nutricosmetics leaders (collagen, gummies, hair) ignore it. This is the white space. It must be claimed with **credibility codes** (named ingredient, compliant dose, vegan/oil format, French expert voices), because the "12 mg / rajeunir de 10 ans" route collides with the EU 8 mg cap and claim rules.

### Inferences (candidate positionings for the strategist)
1. **"La marque de l'astaxanthine" (mono-ingredient, beauty-first)**: a premium, design-led brand fully dedicated to astaxanthin for skin ageing, aimed at women 35-55. Unclaimed. Proof stack: named ingredient (AstaReal®, AstaPure® or NatAxtin®), oil softgel (ideally vegan), H. pluvialis traceability, skin-study citations, 60-day guarantee.
2. **"Protection cellulaire / éclat 365 jours" rather than "préparateur solaire"**: competitors only activate the sun angle seasonally. A year-round "photo-vieillissement / bouclier antioxydant de la peau" story is weakly executed. It must avoid "remplace la crème solaire".
3. **"Le pigment rose" brand world**: pink/coral visual codes (salmon, flamingo, microalgae), clearly apart from white-and-blue lab packaging and gummy pastels. The metaphor is widely used in text but no brand owns it visually. This is an inference: packaging visuals were not observed.
4. **Dose transparency without regulatory exposure**: "dosage maximal autorisé en Europe (8 mg)" plus synergists (vitamin E, vitamin C, zinc) can beat anonymous 12 mg listings on trust. Keeping 12 mg means competing in the Amazon "Forte Puissance" arena, with higher compliance and ad-platform risk.
5. **French expert and UGC ecosystem**: no Venesson/Mohamed-type voice owns astaxanthin. A pharmacist or dermatologist ambassador plus French before/after UGC (4-8 weeks, the timeframe the category itself uses) is open ground.
